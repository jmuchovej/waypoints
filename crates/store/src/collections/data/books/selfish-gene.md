Dawkins reframes evolution from the organism's perspective down to the gene's. The core insight — that bodies are just vehicles genes build to propagate themselves — is one of those ideas that feels obvious in hindsight but was genuinely radical at publication. He's a gifted explainer and the prose carries you along even through the game theory chapters.

The "meme" chapter at the end feels almost like an afterthought, which is ironic given how much cultural weight that word now carries. The rest of the book is tighter and more carefully argued than people who only know Dawkins from Twitter might expect.

Worth reading alongside something like Complexity or The Extended Phenotype. The gene-centric view is powerful but not the whole story, and Dawkins himself acknowledges this more than his critics give him credit for.
