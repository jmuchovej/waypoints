Nobel laureate (physics, 2020) and one of the most creative mathematical physicists alive. His work on black holes with Hawking is legendary, but the reading-list relevance is more about his books — The Emperor's New Mind and The Road to Reality.

He has a controversial but fascinating stance that consciousness is non-computable, rooted in Godel's incompleteness theorems and quantum gravity. You don't have to agree with him to find the argument sharpening. The Road to Reality is basically a guided tour of all of mathematical physics — intimidating but rewarding.
