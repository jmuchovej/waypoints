Astronomer, planetary scientist, and probably the most effective science communicator of the 20th century. Cosmos (both the book and the series) made an entire generation care about science. His gift was connecting the grandeur of the universe to everyday human experience without it feeling cheesy.

The Demon-Haunted World is the other essential one — a passionate defense of scientific thinking and skepticism that feels more relevant now than when he wrote it. Sagan genuinely believed the public could handle real science, and he was right.
