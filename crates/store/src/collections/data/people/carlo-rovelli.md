Italian theoretical physicist, one of the founders of loop quantum gravity. His pop-sci writing is unusually poetic — less "here's the math" and more "here's why it matters for how you think about reality."

Known for making quantum gravity accessible without dumbing it down. If you want to feel something while learning physics, Rovelli is your guy.
