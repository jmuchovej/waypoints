The "paradigm shift" guy — though what he actually meant by that is more nuanced than how most people use it. The Structure of Scientific Revolutions changed how we think about science itself: not as a steady march of progress but as long stretches of normal science punctuated by disruptive revolutions.

Essential reading for anyone interested in how knowledge actually changes over time. His ideas about incommensurability between paradigms are still debated and still relevant.
