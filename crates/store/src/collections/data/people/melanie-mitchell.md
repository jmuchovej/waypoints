Complexity researcher at the Santa Fe Institute, student of Hofstadter. She bridges the gap between complexity science and AI in a way that's remarkably clear-headed — no hype, no doom, just careful thinking about what these systems actually do.

Her book on complexity is one of the best single-volume introductions to the field. Also writes well about why analogy-making might be more central to intelligence than we usually assume.
