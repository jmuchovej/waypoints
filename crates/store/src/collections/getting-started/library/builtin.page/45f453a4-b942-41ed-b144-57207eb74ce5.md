Waypoints is a structured editor for capturing and organizing your work. Everything you write lives inside *Marks* — discrete, identifiable blocks of content that can be referenced, linked, and composed.

<!-- wp:mark 1c4730da-9111-4001-8a33-ee92d854ee91 -->
::callout{theme-default icon="💡"}
<!-- wp:mark bd2f7d4d-9120-426f-a22e-dc950ebdc955 -->
Type `/` at the start of any block to open the slash menu and convert it to a different type — heading, list, blockquote, callout, code, and more.
::
<!-- /wp:mark -->

## Formatting

<!-- wp:mark 8b302088-b5ff-4d64-b195-6db087d34ed0 -->
Inline styles: *bold*, _italic_, *underline*, ~~strikethrough~~, and `inline code`. You can also use superscript and ~~subscript~~ for technical notation, or \|\|spoilers\|\| to hide sensitive content until revealed.

<!-- wp:mark 3e5e3e9e-63fa-40a7-8ee5-3bf05ea9f407 -->
> <!-- wp:mark 406039c2-cdf9-4baa-a4b2-cf57830da2aa -->
> Blockquotes pull out key ideas or reference external sources. Nest them for layered commentary.
<!-- /wp:mark -->

<!-- wp:mark aaad61be-c947-48d7-94b8-508273cb4b59 -->
::callout{theme-default icon="ℹ️"}
<!-- wp:mark cbec2acc-afa1-4232-8a36-8c30866993ac -->
Callouts draw attention to important information. They come in five flavors: note, tip, important, warning, and caution.
::
<!-- /wp:mark -->

<!-- wp:mark 4fb334cf-dce8-4f84-bf4b-099186d90c24 -->
### Headings

<!-- wp:mark 0a98d675-2af7-4bd9-8537-f0e4d702df05 -->
Three levels of headings help structure your marks. Use them to create a scannable hierarchy within longer documents.

<!-- wp:mark 8d9fc4b4-dcba-4090-9155-6ac228599348 -->
### Code Blocks

<!-- wp:mark 200b8af2-8cb6-40b1-b92e-ede2d779307a -->
Embed code with language-specific syntax highlighting:

<!-- wp:mark 6bb0099f-2afc-478b-b32a-5f7df7d3d636 -->
```rust
fn main() {
    let waypoint = Mark::new("hello-world");
    waypoint.publish();
}
```

<!-- wp:mark 2ad54f3c-abf0-457b-bafc-bf348e2689b2 -->
### Lists

<!-- wp:mark 2d933ad9-5af6-4f4b-8da7-75a1392da020 -->
- Marks are the atomic unit of content

<!-- wp:mark 090f2596-3963-4fa1-9fa6-a4b29df951d8 -->
- Each mark gets a unique identifier

<!-- wp:mark bc0d9908-cbe2-4490-a09f-837f2aa7f496 -->
- Nested items organize sub-points

<!-- wp:mark 92ab6024-5653-43a6-8e90-414d2a6b50a9 -->
- Indentation conveys hierarchy

<!-- wp:mark 17d27713-2449-44f9-9fd6-20a768dc7fd2 -->
+ Numbered lists track ordered sequences

<!-- wp:mark 0434e90d-f7b6-47d1-b994-40082bfef59e -->
+ Use them for steps, rankings, or progressions

## Keyboard Shortcuts

<!-- wp:mark 365ef508-688c-4e1a-8f14-5f35074397fa -->
Most formatting is a keystroke away:

<!-- wp:mark 61df0596-6741-44aa-8b56-4b868411af43 -->
- *Bold* — `Cmd+B`

<!-- wp:mark 5c793bdb-1516-46d5-ae6b-a0986efcee55 -->
- *Italic* — `Cmd+I`

<!-- wp:mark a82af4a2-f652-476e-ac8b-e5753d867a4e -->
- *Underline* — `Cmd+U`

<!-- wp:mark 0355cd05-f571-429b-94f2-e49861e7ad04 -->
- *Inline Code* — `Cmd+E`

<!-- wp:mark 131793e6-f6a1-43ab-a895-a09e78b14a87 -->
- *Strikethrough* — `Cmd+Shift+S`

<!-- wp:mark 8117afb6-7fc8-4c03-a5f6-d461fb0628d6 -->
- *Spoiler* — `Cmd+Shift+H`

<!-- wp:mark 36cbe918-5879-436b-ab83-6fe12687156c -->
- *Highlight* — `Cmd+Shift+E`

<!-- wp:mark 015074ab-49dc-4307-aa0c-4637db344010 -->
- *Superscript* — `Cmd+.`

<!-- wp:mark 7dbdc737-0d91-4b4a-ad86-4540577b10a3 -->
- *Subscript* — `Cmd+,`

<!-- wp:mark 80378c58-756f-42d4-8e51-184e1dbe98e7 -->
::callout{theme-default icon="❗"}
<!-- wp:mark dbe9fc38-30bb-491a-b0c5-13a5b4a85fd3 -->
Use `@` to mention other marks inline. Mentions are live references — click them to navigate, hover for a preview.
::
<!-- /wp:mark -->
