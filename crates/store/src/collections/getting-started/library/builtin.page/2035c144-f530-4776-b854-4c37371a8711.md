## Itinerary

<!-- wp:mark 0e7197d6-fb0d-48a0-8ce1-98b6b8f85f5a -->
We're spending *two weeks* in Japan, split between Tokyo, Kyoto, and a few days exploring the countryside. The rough plan:

<!-- wp:mark 306034bc-9651-4ff0-bec2-58dde95236b7 -->
+ Days 1–4: Tokyo — Shibuya, Akihabara, Tsukiji Outer Market, day trip to Kamakura

<!-- wp:mark 89848b44-9c90-488a-8afb-fba6afc85b84 -->
+ Days 5–7: Hakone — ryokan stay, Open Air Museum, views of Fuji

<!-- wp:mark 6b54c712-853a-4db9-b5a3-0d2e122db372 -->
+ Days 8–11: Kyoto — Fushimi Inari, Arashiyama bamboo grove, tea ceremony

<!-- wp:mark b02b3171-4bd2-48de-9cc2-5c5c46e786c2 -->
+ Days 12–14: Osaka + Nara — street food in Dotonbori, deer park, Osaka Castle

## Transport

<!-- wp:mark badf4913-ff68-4b0c-8964-e5b823c03272 -->
The *Japan Rail Pass* (14-day) is the obvious choice. Covers all JR lines including the Shinkansen between cities. A few notes:

<!-- wp:mark 1ff47d5d-22e3-498e-ba5b-2e5c5bd1dad0 -->
- Activate it on day 1 at Narita or Haneda

<!-- wp:mark 48cb15eb-4b9e-44f0-b96a-10ce73e15a53 -->
- _Does not_ cover the Nozomi or Mizuho trains — use Hikari instead

<!-- wp:mark 51af1128-797c-48fe-9108-a91f69142d55 -->
- Local metros in Tokyo and Kyoto need a separate IC card (`Suica` or `ICOCA`)

<!-- wp:mark 556056bb-842c-4f0e-8b1f-a65e6b69507f -->
- Airport limousine bus from Narita to Shinjuku is ~3,000 yen and sometimes faster than the train

<!-- wp:mark 336d3ab7-1c62-41ac-b525-a7a97f150fd0 -->
> <!-- wp:mark 9d6433aa-1e3f-4f27-aaaa-0f8148811963 -->
> Pro tip: the JR Pass also covers the ferry to Miyajima if you extend to Hiroshima.
<!-- /wp:mark -->

## Budget Estimates

<!-- wp:mark 3c64c49e-dff6-48c9-a190-57c604675d55 -->
Rough per-person daily budget in yen, assuming mid-range travel:

<!-- wp:mark 0bc04398-fd13-4717-b78e-70d67aeabde8 -->
- *Accommodation*: ¥8,000–12,000 (mix of hostels and business hotels)

<!-- wp:mark aaf3d23b-2482-43b6-a445-95cb88b50c7d -->
- *Food*: ¥3,000–5,000 (convenience stores for breakfast, sit-down for dinner)

<!-- wp:mark 767c5c3c-07bf-4c5d-b5be-e1de04b9ac70 -->
- *Transport*: covered by JR Pass + ~¥1,000/day for local transit

<!-- wp:mark 04f04d39-6299-40a5-9fe8-fa4fe8d48846 -->
- *Activities*: ¥1,500–3,000 (temple entries, museums)

<!-- wp:mark 310262ed-d91f-4ca5-97c0-9d5960b4208e -->
Total for 14 days: roughly *¥200,000–280,000* per person (~1,900 USD).

## Packing Notes

<!-- wp:mark 8644d32c-63e3-42a9-9766-0073cdf71739 -->
It's a spring trip, so expect variable weather. Key items:

<!-- wp:mark b646e5ba-7e7b-41d9-ae63-e5c02594063f -->
- Layers — mornings can be ~~cold~~ chilly, afternoons warm

<!-- wp:mark e1d2b8a4-97c4-41a1-b1c9-38883b6895cc -->
- Comfortable walking shoes (we'll average 15,000+ steps/day)

<!-- wp:mark a2fd3365-87fb-4eb1-b111-f4a9809d90d7 -->
- Portable battery pack for phones

<!-- wp:mark 252751eb-808e-4828-bce7-0bea97968e25 -->
- A small packable daypack for day trips

<!-- wp:mark 4090ffa4-af03-45e1-9b7f-735bb0b8c450 -->
- Rain jacket or compact umbrella — cherry blossom season overlaps with spring showers

<!-- wp:mark e48f76a1-237b-45e4-b36b-8afcd0f85483 -->
Leave room in the suitcase for souvenirs. Japanese `konbini` (convenience stores) sell basically everything you might forget.
