The self-reference guy. Best known for GEB, which is one of those books people either worship or bounce off of hard. His core obsession is how meaning and consciousness emerge from loops and self-referential structures — minds modeling themselves.

<!-- wp:mark 66a17b0b-6300-4d67-8275-ae52d170c9f9 -->
He's genuinely unusual in that he sits at the intersection of math, AI, music, and philosophy and somehow makes all of it feel like one subject. Not always easy reading, but always worth the effort.
