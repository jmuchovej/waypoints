Philosopher of science, best known for co-writing Godel's Proof with James Newman. That little book is one of the most successful attempts to make Godel's incompleteness theorems genuinely understandable to non-mathematicians.

<!-- wp:mark dd6119b2-7a89-46cd-aff8-ec8cc33e20eb -->
His broader work in philosophy of science is solid logical empiricism — careful, rigorous, not flashy. The kind of thinker who makes you more precise without making you more confused.
