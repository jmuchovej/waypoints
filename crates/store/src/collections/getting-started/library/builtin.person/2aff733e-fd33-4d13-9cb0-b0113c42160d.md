String theorist and one of the better physics popularizers working today. The Elegant Universe is probably the most accessible intro to string theory out there — he's good at building up from relativity and quantum mechanics to show why people think extra dimensions might be real.

<!-- wp:mark 151dfd24-bac2-4167-ae44-73c581e2cd68 -->
His later books cast a wider net (cosmology, time, meaning of it all). Solid at making hard physics visual and intuitive, even when the underlying math is way out of reach.
