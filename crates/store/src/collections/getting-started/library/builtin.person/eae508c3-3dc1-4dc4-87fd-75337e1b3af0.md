Nobel laureate, legendary teacher, and probably the most naturally gifted physics communicator who ever lived. His Lectures on Physics are still the gold standard for building real physical intuition — decades later, nothing has replaced them.

<!-- wp:mark 1ab2819e-96a3-4d41-a487-0b8574598285 -->
The autobiographical stuff (Surely You're Joking, etc.) is fun but the real treasure is how he explains things. He had this relentless drive to understand from first principles and an allergic reaction to jargon that hid confusion.
