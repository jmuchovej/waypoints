Israeli historian who went from medieval military history to writing the biggest-picture books imaginable. Sapiens is the main event — a sweeping narrative about how Homo sapiens took over the world, with the core argument that our superpower is collective fiction (money, religion, nations, etc.).

<!-- wp:mark 3e0c14ff-b7b9-41ea-a7bc-0cfc348cd6cd -->
Polarizing among specialists (historians tend to nitpick the details), but the high-level framing is genuinely useful. He's at his best when he makes you question things you assumed were just "natural."
