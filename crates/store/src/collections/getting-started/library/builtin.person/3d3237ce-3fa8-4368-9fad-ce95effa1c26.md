Science writer and mathematician, editor of the massive four-volume anthology The World of Mathematics. Also co-authored Godel's Proof with Ernest Nagel, which remains one of the clearest short treatments of the incompleteness theorems.

<!-- wp:mark 04eb1b2a-4424-4ae4-adc2-461df2971156 -->
Newman was more of a curating and explaining mind than an original researcher — but that's exactly what makes him valuable here. He had great taste in what mattered and a knack for making it land.
