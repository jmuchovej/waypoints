Evolutionary biologist who basically invented the gene's-eye view of evolution for a popular audience. The Selfish Gene is a landmark — it reframes how you think about natural selection in a way that sticks.

His earlier work (through Blind Watchmaker era) is where the real clarity lives. Later stuff gets more polemical, but the biology writing is genuinely excellent at building intuition for how evolution actually works.
