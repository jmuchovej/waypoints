Waypoints is a block-based knowledge tool that keeps your notes on your own device.

Every heading, paragraph, list, callout and code block is its own block, with its own identity. Move one and the links pointing at it still resolve. The text around it is left alone.

PAGES WITH A SHAPE

A Chart describes which fields a page carries, and every page built from that Chart keeps them typed — dates stay dates, numbers stay numbers. Charts ship for pages, notes, people, meetings, campaigns, objectives, bookmarks, journals and books, and you can define your own. Below the fields, the page is still a page: the same blocks, in the same editor.

LINKS THAT POINT BOTH WAYS

A link is a real edge with a direction and a kind, not a string in your text. A page can show what points back at it. Two pages can be joined more than once, each connection keeping its own kind, so neither overwrites the other.

YOUR DEVICE, AND NOWHERE ELSE

There is no account to create and no API key to paste. Your notes live in a database on your device. Waypoints does not phone home, and there is no telemetry to switch off.

EARLY DEVELOPMENT

Waypoints is early software under active development. Several things you might expect from a mature notes app are not here yet, among them full-text search, saved views and filters, importing from other note apps, and syncing notes between your devices. If you need those today, Waypoints is not ready for you — but if you want to watch a local-first knowledge tool take shape, you are in the right place.
