# Tasks

All work lands on one branch and reaches `main` as a single merge commit (design.md D8). The groups are in branch order: the tree is made to conform before anything enforces conformance, so every commit is green under the existing hooks and the validator needs no advisory mode. The copyright holder is `John Muchovej`; a file's copyright year is the year it was first authored and never changes (design.md D3). Groups 4 and 5 are holder-independent and may be developed in parallel, then ordered after group 3. External contributions stay closed after the merge, until a follow-on change delivers the CLA portal.

That change carries the scenarios of _Every contribution lands with recorded assent to a CLA version_, reopens contributions, and updates _External contributions are closed until the CLA portal is live_.
- Steward and counsel review the flags recorded in design.md Appendix B, confirm or change Connecticut, write the enforcement-intent statement (Appendix D) and the plugin-boundary note (Appendix E), choose the Creative Commons option for documentation and website copy, and decide the content-and-brand carve-out (design.md Open Questions).
- The Steward signs a §7.3(b) designation naming a successor on death or Incapacity, records its existence and date in `docs/licensing/STEWARD.md`, and executes the companion instrument the Incapacity definition relies on.
- Operational succession beside the legal one: a second owner on the GitHub organisation, the domain registrar, the Apple developer account, and Turso, which will hold the signature records a successor needs.
- Once branch rulesets exist, add the `license` job, and the portal's status once it exists, to the required checks, and with them a `docs/CODEOWNERS` naming the Steward for the licensing surface — `LICENSE`, `docs/licensing/`, `.config/third-party.toml`, `.config/deny.toml`, the workspace `Cargo.toml` and `src/nuxt.config.ts` — with "require review from code owners" on the ruleset. It only does anything once a second person holds merge rights (the rule is enforced by the ruleset, not the file, and today the Steward is the sole merger), so it lands with the rulesets rather than here; it governs who approves a change to those files, which the validator cannot, and mitigates none of the mechanical risks the validator already catches.
- At archive, copy each capability's dated decisions from design.md's "Spec decisions" into its new main spec as a `## Decisions` section, the way `openspec/specs/storage/spec.md` carries its own; the archive step does not carry a delta's extra sections.
- Archive the change after the merge; the portal is its own change and need not wait for this one to be archived.
